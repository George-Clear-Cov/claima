street address, then **every attribution re-checked against the practice's NPPES org NPI.**

only link available — which means shared medical buildings must be caught by hand. Five were.

**Figures recomputed 2026-09-09.** Every Tier A range is derived from the `allowed`
charge printed in its own email: `allowed / 0.30 × (47/365) × 9%–25%`, fee
`(lo×0.31 + hi×0.52)/2 × 30%`. ONS and Litchfield Hills previously carried ranges
computed from a pre-NPPES-recheck `allowed`, so the table and the email body disagreed.
