## Long / boilerplate (~160 words — press, deck, About page)
Claima is an AI-native revenue cycle management (RCM) platform built for outpatient medical practices. Practices lose a significant share of revenue to claim denials, underpayments, and manual billing errors, and their only options are expensive in-house billers or opaque RCM firms. Claima automates the entire revenue cycle — insurance eligibility verification, AI-powered claim scrubbing, submission, denial recovery with AI-drafted appeals, remittance posting, and patient billing — and extends into prior authorization, provider credentialing, chronic care management, compliance screening, and payer-level intelligence. Because Claima is priced on a percentage of what it collects, its incentives are aligned with the practice: it only wins when the practice gets paid. The platform is built on Anthropic Claude via Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Azure, and is fully HIPAA-compliant with executed Business Associate Agreements, encryption, multi-factor authentication, and audit logging. Claima helps practices of every specialty recover denied claims, reduce administrative burden, and get paid faster.
