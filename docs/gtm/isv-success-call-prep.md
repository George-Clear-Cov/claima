# ISV Success Manager — Intro Call Prep

> Goal of the call: get the EM to (1) plug you into the **Health & Life Sciences co-sell motion**,
> (2) unlock your **benefits** (credits, technical consultations, Marketplace Rewards), and (3) commit
> to **named intros + next steps**. Lead with a crisp pitch, pivot fast to your asks, leave with commitments.
> Positioning = full AI-native RCM platform ([[feedback-positioning]]).

## 30-second pitch (say this first)
"Claima is an **AI-native revenue cycle management platform** for outpatient medical practices. We
automate the full billing lifecycle — eligibility, AI claim scrubbing, submission, **denial recovery
with AI-drafted appeals**, and patient billing — and we charge a **percentage of what we collect**, so
we only win when the practice gets paid. We're **HIPAA-compliant, built on Azure** with Claude on AWS
Bedrock, **live on AppSource**, and landing our first pilot on denial recovery."

## Traction snapshot (your proof points — have these ready)
- Full platform **built + deployed**, HIPAA-compliant on **Azure** (verified TLS, MFA, audit logging)
- **Live on Microsoft AppSource** — co-sell "In Market"
- Clearinghouse (Claim.MD) integration **validated live**; athenahealth integration validated in sandbox
- **BAAs executed** — AWS, Azure, Claim.MD
- **First pilot verbally secured** (denial-recovery engagement)
- Claima LLC; **pre-seed raise in progress** (~$1.5M SAFE)
- Azure footprint growing (App Service, Postgres, Key Vault, ACS) — attributable consumption

## Your top asks (this is the point of the call — prioritized)
1. **Intro to the Health & Life Sciences ISV team / a vertical PDM.** Your #1 ask — the health team is
   who co-sells health ISVs. Ask for a name + warm intro.
2. **Co-sell activation.** "What makes an offer co-sell-ready beyond 'In Market,' and how do I get
   Microsoft field sellers representing Claima?" Ask how to register co-sell opportunities in Referrals
   and the path to **IP co-sell** later.
3. **Maximize Azure credits.** How do **ISV Success + Microsoft for Startups** credits stack? What's the
   roadmap to build attributable Azure consumption (Azure OpenAI fast tier, App Insights, Blob for EDI
   archive, Document Intelligence for paper EOBs) toward the Startups milestones?
4. **Marketplace Rewards.** Activate co-marketing + listing optimization now that you're live.
5. **Technical consultations.** Book an **Azure architecture + HIPAA security** review (a benefit you have).
6. **Marketplace/MACC path.** When should we stand up a **transactable** offer so enterprise health
   systems can procure via marketplace and spend their Azure commitment (MACC)?

## Questions to ask the EM
- Who's my path to the **Health & Life Sciences** vertical team / a PDM, and can you make the intro?
- Beyond "In Market," what do I need to get **field sellers** actively co-selling Claima?
- What **credits** am I eligible for across ISV Success + Startups, and how do they stack?
- Which **technical consultations** should I book first?
- What triggers a **dedicated Engagement Manager** vs. self-serve — and what's your role for me?
- Any **health-tech events / co-marketing** I should be in (HLTH, ViVE, Ignite)?
- What's the current guidance on **App Accelerate** (the new 2026 unified program)?

## Have ready / bring
- Pitch deck or one-pager (if you have it); if not, the traction snapshot above
- Your **top-3 asks** written down (so you steer the call)
- Your Azure usage + near-term roadmap (to align credits)

## How to run it → outcomes to leave with
1. Pitch (30 sec) → "Here's what I'm hoping you can help with" → your asks.
2. **Get specifics before you hang up:** a name for the health PDM intro, which consultations to book,
   which credits to claim, and a **follow-up date**.
3. Send a **same-day recap email** listing the agreed next steps + owners (keeps the EM accountable).

## Don't
- Don't let it become a generic "here's how ISV Success works" tour — you've read the program; drive to asks.
- Don't undersell as "medical billing" — you're a **full RCM platform**; denial recovery is the wedge.
- Don't wait for them to volunteer help — ask directly for the intros + bookings.
