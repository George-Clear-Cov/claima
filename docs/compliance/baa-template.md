This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by **[PRACTICE LEGAL NAME]**
("Covered Entity") and **[CLAIMA LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("Business Associate"), effective
**[DATE]**. It governs Business Associate's handling of Protected Health Information ("PHI")
under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, the HITECH Act, and their
implementing regulations (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164) ("HIPAA Rules").

## 1. Definitions
Terms used but not defined have the meaning in the HIPAA Rules. "PHI" means Protected
Health Information created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate on
Covered Entity's behalf.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures
Business Associate may use/disclose PHI only:
(a) to perform the services in the underlying service agreement (revenue-cycle / denial
management), consistent with **minimum necessary**;
(b) for Business Associate's proper management and administration or legal responsibilities;
(c) to provide data aggregation services to Covered Entity as permitted by 45 CFR §164.504(e)(2)(i)(B);
(d) to create de-identified information per 45 CFR §164.514(a)-(b) (Safe Harbor), which upon
de-identification is no longer PHI and not subject to this Agreement.
Business Associate will not use/disclose PHI in any manner that would violate the HIPAA
Rules if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted in (b)-(d).

## 3. Obligations of Business Associate
Business Associate shall:
(a) not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted here or required by law;
(b) implement administrative, physical, and technical **safeguards** (45 CFR §164.308, .310,
.312) that reasonably and appropriately protect PHI, and comply with the Security Rule with
respect to ePHI;
(c) **report** to Covered Entity any use/disclosure not permitted here, any Security
Incident, and any Breach of Unsecured PHI, without unreasonable delay and no later than
**[NUMBER — e.g., 5] business days** after discovery, including the information required by
45 CFR §164.410;
(d) ensure any **subcontractor** that creates/receives/maintains/transmits PHI on its behalf
agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions (flow-down);
(e) make PHI available to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations for **access** (§164.524) and
**amendment** (§164.526), and provide an **accounting of disclosures** (§164.528);
(f) make its internal practices, books, and records available to the **Secretary of HHS** for
determining compliance;
(g) to the extent Business Associate carries out a Covered Entity obligation, comply with the
Privacy Rule requirements applicable to that obligation.

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity
Covered Entity shall: notify Business Associate of any limitation(s) in its Notice of Privacy
Practices, any changes to or revocation of an individual's authorization, and any restriction
on use/disclosure Covered Entity has agreed to or is required to abide by, to the extent such
affects Business Associate's use/disclosure of PHI. Covered Entity shall not request
Business Associate to use/disclose PHI in a manner not permitted by the HIPAA Rules.

## 5. Term and termination
(a) **Term:** effective on the date above and continues until all PHI is returned or destroyed,
or protections are extended per (c).
(b) **Termination for cause:** upon Business Associate's material breach, Covered Entity may
provide an opportunity to cure and terminate if not cured, or terminate immediately if cure
is not feasible.
(c) **Effect of termination:** upon termination, Business Associate shall return or destroy
all PHI (including PHI held by subcontractors). Where return/destruction is infeasible,
Business Associate shall extend the protections of this Agreement to such PHI and limit
further use/disclosure to the purposes that make return/destruction infeasible.

## 6. Miscellaneous
(a) **Regulatory references** are to the applicable section as in effect or amended.
(b) **Amendment:** the parties will amend this Agreement as necessary to comply with the
HIPAA Rules.
(c) **Interpretation:** ambiguities are resolved to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules.
(d) **No third-party beneficiaries.** (e) **Survival:** Section 5(c) survives termination.
(f) **Governing law:** [STATE], excluding conflict-of-laws.
